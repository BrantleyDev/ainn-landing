About AINN

AINN (Apple Intelligence News Network) is an independent technology-news project focused on tracking and summarizing developments related to Apple and its surrounding technology ecosystem. It is currently in active development.

What this project does

AINN researches publicly available technology news, prepares summaries and draft content, and — where enabled and manually approved by its operator — shares that content on connected platforms. No content is published automatically without human review.

Status

This project is currently in development and testing. This page exists to provide the transparency information required by third-party platform integrations (such as identity and API providers) while AINN is being built.

Contact

For questions about this project, contact the operator through the project's GitHub repository.
